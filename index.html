Stage I: Unit 4 Review & Integrated Support Comparison

Diagnostic & Synthesis

Quick Unit 4 Concept Review (5 mins)

Synthesizing: Emotional Intelligence (Plans 1-2), Healthy Sexuality & Relationships (Plans 3-4), and Addiction & Prevention (Plans 5-6).

1. Emotional Distress

Signs: Persistent anxiety, sudden withdrawal, emotional outbursts.

Support Strategy:

Active listening, validation without judgment, suggesting grounding techniques (5-4-3-2-1), and escorting to school counselor.

2. Unsafe Relationships

Signs: Boundary violations, coercion, digital harassment, isolation.

Support Strategy:

Reaffirming body autonomy, helping draft clear boundary scripts, involving trusted adults immediately for safety.

3. Addiction Concerns

Signs: Loss of control, academic collapse, secrecy, behavioral changes.

Support Strategy:

Removing shame/moral judgment, expressing care for their health, referring to professional helplines or medical support.

Stage II: Empathetic & Non-judgmental Responses

Roleplay & Scenario Practice

Practice applying Montano Habit 5 (Seek First to Understand) using non-judgmental communication scripts in peer support scenarios.

Scenario 1: Friend Experiencing Severe Stress

Helpful Script:

"I notice you've been feeling overwhelmed lately. I'm here to listen without judging. Would you like to talk or walk to the counselor's office together?"

Avoid saying: "Just cheer up, it's not a big deal."

Scenario 2: Peer Facing Digital Boundary Violation

Helpful Script:

"Your privacy matter and you didn't deserve that. Let's document this and speak to a teacher or parent right now."

Scenario 3: Peer Showing Loss of Control

Helpful Script:

"I care about you and I've noticed you seem really stressed and exhausted. You don't have to carry this alone—there are professionals who can help."

Scenario 4: When Safety is at Risk

Golden Rule:

Peers offer empathy, NOT therapy or crisis intervention. If a friend is in immediate danger or harming themselves, break secrecy and tell a trusted adult immediately.

Stage III: Personal Well-being & Prevention Action Plan

Individual Action Blueprint

"Sharpen the Saw" Personal Action Plan

Complete your 4-Pillar Action Plan in your student workbook:

1. Emotional Regulation Goal "When I feel overwhelmed, I will use Box Breathing for 2 minutes before reacting."

4. Designated Trusted Network "My 2 trusted adults for school and personal support are [Counselor/Parent/Teacher]."
